On 6 May 2026 from 16:00–19:00 at JKU – LIT Open Innovation Center, Young AI Leaders Austria hosted Idea Reality Check—a 3-hour, hands-on session to take an idea, validate it, and turn it into a working prototype.

## What happened

Participants sharpened ideas worth pursuing, pressure-tested them quickly, and built first prototypes using Lovable. No polished concept was required—half-finished thoughts were welcome. The session was led by Maximilian Momo, who leads an MBA in Applied AI & Business Innovation at Privatuniversität Schloss Seeburg and has implemented AI across organisations and ventures from banking to industry.

## Why it matters

People do not need another talk about AI; they need focused hours that produce something tangible. Idea Reality Check turns inspiration into applied practice.
