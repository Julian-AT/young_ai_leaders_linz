Young AI Leaders Austria teamed up with Junge Wirtschaft Uni Linz for an evening on practical AI—focused on building a personal AI assistant that actually saves time at work.

## What happened

Keynote speaker Alexander Schurr (Co-Founder, Linzer KI Company GmbH; FH Hagenberg lecturer; TEDx speaker) broke down concrete tools participants could use immediately. The programme emphasised hands-on AI for daily routines and real-world insight from a local AI founder—not abstract trends.

## Why it matters

Most professionals do not need another high-level AI overview. They need workflows that fit a real workday. Formats like this translate Linz’s AI community into immediate productivity and literacy gains.
