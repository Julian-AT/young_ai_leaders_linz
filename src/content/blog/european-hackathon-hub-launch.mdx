On Saturday, 20 June 2026, from 18:00 to 21:00 CEST, Young AI Leaders Austria joined a simultaneous European launch moment for a new chapter in Europe’s hackathon ecosystem.

## What happened

Nine cities across six countries launched together: London, Paris, Vienna, Budapest, Munich, Espoo, Nuremberg, Kassel, and Linz. The evening brought builders, organisers, student societies, communities, and partners into a shared live moment—with more forming behind the scenes for those ready to get involved early.

## Why it matters

Europe’s hackathon culture is stronger when cities coordinate instead of competing in isolation. Linz’s place in a nine-city launch signals that Upper Austria is part of the continent’s builder infrastructure from day one.
