Young AI Leaders Austria expanded its student–industry track through collaboration with WKOÖ’s KI\*Transfer programme—linking AI learners with companies ready to explore practical applications.

## What happened

KI\*Transfer supports the movement of AI know-how into Upper Austrian businesses. Together with the Linz hub, the focus is applied projects: students work on real briefs, companies gain structured experimentation capacity, and both sides build a shared language for responsible AI adoption.

## Why it matters

Regional competitiveness depends on transfer, not only research excellence. Pairing WKOÖ’s industry reach with Young AI Leaders Austria’s talent network shortens the path from classroom capability to workplace impact.
