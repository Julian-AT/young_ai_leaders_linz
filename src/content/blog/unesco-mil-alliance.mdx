Young AI Leaders Austria was officially confirmed as a member of the UNESCO Media and Information Literacy Alliance—connecting the Linz hub’s AI literacy work to a global network spanning more than 100 countries.

## What happened

While public debate often jumps to autonomous agents, many participants in literacy workshops still ask basic questions: Is this video real? Can I trust what an AI tells me? The gap is literacy, not access. An Informatica study with Deloitte across the US, UK/EU, and APAC found that while 69% of organisations have embedded GenAI, 74% of the workforce lack AI literacy to use it responsibly.

## Why it matters

UNESCO MIL Alliance membership links local workshops in Linz to international policy and practice on media and information literacy—reinforcing that responsible AI use is a civic skill, not only a technical one.
