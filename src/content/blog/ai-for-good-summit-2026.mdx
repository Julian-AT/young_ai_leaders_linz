The AI generation is not waiting for directions—it is already building. In July 2026, that generation is again part of the conversation at the United Nations’ [AI for Good Global Summit](https://aiforgood.itu.int/) in Geneva.

## What happened

Young AI Leaders Austria carries into the summit a year-plus of Linz hub practice: local community programmes, youth-led AI initiatives, AI literacy formats, robotics and STEAM education, hackathons, and practical tools for places where access and resources are limited. The summit is where those local experiments meet the global AI for Good agenda.

## Why it matters

International stages matter most when they are fed by real community work. Geneva is not a departure from Linz—it is the amplification of what Young AI Leaders Austria builds at home.
