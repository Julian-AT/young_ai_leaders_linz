Young AI Leaders Austria became a member of the [Austrian Society for Artificial Intelligence (ASAI)](https://www.asai.ac.at/en/)—Austria’s central professional association supporting AI research collaboration between universities and industry.

## What happened

ASAI membership places the Linz hub inside the country’s core AI research and industry network. It strengthens ties between youth programmes and the academic–industrial collaborations that shape Austria’s AI capability.

## Why it matters

A durable talent pipeline needs professional institutions as well as community events. ASAI connects Young AI Leaders Austria to the national research fabric that underpins long-term AI excellence.
