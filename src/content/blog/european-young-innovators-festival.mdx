Young AI Leaders Austria served as a jury member at the European Young Innovators Festival in Graz, organised by WSA, where promising young founders presented digital solutions with real social impact.

## What happened

The festival highlights Europe’s next generation of impact-driven digital ventures. Representing the Linz hub on the jury meant evaluating ideas not only for technical ambition, but for whether they can serve people and communities in practice.

## Why it matters

Youth innovation ecosystems grow stronger when hubs cross borders to mentor and assess—not only to host. Jury service connects Linz to a wider European pipeline of founders building technology for good.
