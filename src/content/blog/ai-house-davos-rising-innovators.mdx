One day after the Linz hub launch, Young AI Leaders Austria was represented at AI House Davos in the roundtable “What’s in it for us? Rising innovators’ perspectives on AI.”

## What happened

The session ran on Tuesday, 21 January 2025, from 11:40 to 12:35 in Room “Ada Lovelace” at AI House Davos (Promenade 68, Davos Platz). Felix Rank opened and closed the agenda; participants gave short reports on the state of AI, then moved into open discussion.

Confirmed voices included Anna Spitznagel (trail), Benedikt Gieger (SAP SE), Gabriele Mazzini (architect and lead author of the EU AI Act), Gabby Chan (S2S Ventures), Kiara Sweeney (Yunus Environment Hub), and Linz hub leader Dino Osmanagić, among others from startups, investment, and policy.

## Why it matters

For two decades, young people have grown up with digital technologies. This session put ethics, sustainability, and the future of work under that next-generation lens—and put the Linz hub on a global stage from its first week.
