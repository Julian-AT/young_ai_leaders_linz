Business Upper Austria featured Markus Roth (uBIT) and Dino Osmanagić (Young AI Leaders Austria) in the podcast “KI in der Praxis – aber richtig,” focused on how companies can use AI with real value—from idea to implementation.

## What happened

The conversation covered practical guidance for organisations that want more than experiments: how to move from pilots to outcomes that matter for Upper Austria’s business location. It also set the stage for Zukunft.Digitalisierung on 22 October at Softwarepark Hagenberg, where strategic AI roadmaps would take centre stage.

## Why it matters

Most AI adoption barriers are organisational, not technical. A clear public conversation—in the language of practitioners—helps companies in the region approach AI with structure, realism, and impact in mind.
