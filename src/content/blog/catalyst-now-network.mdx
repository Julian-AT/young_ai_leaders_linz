Young AI Leaders Austria connected with [Catalyst Now](https://catalystnow.org/), the global social innovation network of changemakers and organisations collaborating on systems change for global challenges.

## What happened

Catalyst Now brings together social innovators working across sectors to accelerate progress on shared goals. For the Linz hub, membership opens pathways into international programmes—including Catalyzing Change Week and joint hackathons with NGOs—where AI talent meets authentic social impact briefs.

## Why it matters

AI for Good needs partners who already work on systems change. Catalyst Now widens the Linz hub’s reach beyond tech circles into a worldwide community focused on human-centred, ethical, and inclusive innovation.
