The Global Public Health Hackathon kicked off with a clear mandate: turn urgent health challenges into scalable solutions for access and equity. After more than 500 applications worldwide, 30 teams were selected for 48 hours of focused building.

## What happened

Winning teams were positioned for more than trophies: opportunities to present at the World Health Assembly in Geneva and the WFPHA Global Meeting in Cape Town; technical credits from partners Lovable and n8n; and three months of entrepreneurship mentoring plus ongoing AI and public health support.

Young AI Leaders Austria contributed through the wider health-and-hackathon track connecting Linz talent to global public health innovation.

## Why it matters

Health equity needs builders who can ship under constraints—and pathways that carry prototypes into advocacy and sustained support. This format links weekend intensity to long-term impact.
