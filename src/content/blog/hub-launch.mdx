Young AI Leaders Austria officially launched the Linz hub on 20 January 2025, joining the global [Young AI Leaders community](https://aiforgood.itu.int/young-ai-leaders-community/) under the United Nations’ AI for Good initiative.

## What happened

The launch was confirmed by Alexandra Bustos Iliescu, establishing Linz as an active hub within the worldwide network of young people working on ethical, inclusive, and impact-driven AI. From day one, the focus was clear: build a local community that connects talent, education, industry, and policy around AI for Good.

## Why it matters

A formal hub gives Upper Austria a structured home for youth-led AI engagement—workshops, hackathons, partnerships, and policy dialogue—while linking Linz to peers across continents. That local-to-global connection is the foundation for everything that followed.
