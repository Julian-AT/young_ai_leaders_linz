Young AI Leaders Austria joined the European Commission’s [Apply AI Alliance](https://futurium.ec.europa.eu/en/apply-ai-alliance)—a multi-stakeholder forum facilitating dialogue on AI development, deployment, and societal and economic impact.

## What happened

The Alliance convenes organisations working on how AI is applied in practice across Europe. For the Linz hub, membership extends youth and community perspectives into that EU-level conversation—alongside the hub’s existing engagement in Austrian and UN AI governance spaces.

## Why it matters

Deployment is where principles meet reality. Being present in Apply AI Alliance discussions helps ensure next-generation voices shape how Europe puts AI to work responsibly.
