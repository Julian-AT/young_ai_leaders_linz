Young AI Leaders Austria is now a member of the European Commission’s Pact for Skills—an initiative that brings stakeholders together to develop Europe’s workforce through upskilling and reskilling.

## What happened

Pact membership opens access to resources such as the Guidance Hub (including the skills partnership handbook), the Knowledge Hub with funding search and online library, and the Networking Hub for upcoming events. The Linz hub joins a Europe-wide community sharing milestones and practical approaches to skills development.

## Why it matters

AI literacy programmes land harder when they connect to continental skills policy. Pact for Skills aligns Young AI Leaders Austria with partners committed to making sure no one is left behind as work transforms.
