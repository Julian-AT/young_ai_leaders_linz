Young AI Leaders Austria began collaborating with [Business Upper Austria](https://www.biz-up.at/) and its IT Cluster—Austria’s largest ICT innovation network connecting technology companies, startups, and research institutions in Upper Austria.

## What happened

The partnership opens a direct channel between the Linz hub’s youth community and the regional economy’s digital backbone. It creates a path for joint formats: industry conversations, skills programmes, and practical AI adoption workshops that serve both students and companies.

## Why it matters

AI literacy and talent retention only scale when education and industry move together. Working with the IT Cluster anchors Young AI Leaders Austria in Upper Austria’s innovation system and helps turn community energy into durable regional impact.
