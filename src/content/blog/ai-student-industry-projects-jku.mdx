Young AI Leaders Austria is advancing AI student–industry projects with Johannes Kepler University Linz, the City of Linz, and ÖH AI—meeting a region where AI education is already at European scale.

## What happened

More than 4,000 students are now enrolled in AI programmes at JKU Linz, with roughly 500 new bachelor students in a single semester. That concentration makes Linz one of Europe’s largest AI talent hubs—and creates urgent demand for real-world project pathways into industry and public institutions.

## Why it matters

Talent alone is not enough without structured bridges into companies and civic partners. Student–industry projects turn classroom capability into applied experience and help Upper Austria keep AI talent engaged locally.
