Young AI Leaders Austria joined the [ESF+ Community of Practice](https://socialinnovationplus.eu/) on Employment, Education, and Skills—hosted via the European Competence Centre for Social Innovation.

## What happened

The network supports knowledge sharing on social innovation across employment, education, and skills development. For the Linz hub, it complements AI literacy and talent programmes with a European peer circle focused on how people actually prepare for changing labour markets.

## Why it matters

AI skills agendas succeed when they sit inside broader employment and education policy. ESF+ CoP membership helps Young AI Leaders Austria align local programmes with European social innovation practice.
