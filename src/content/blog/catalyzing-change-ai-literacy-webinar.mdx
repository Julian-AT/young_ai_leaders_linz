As part of Catalyzing Change Week 2025—the world’s largest collaborative event for systems change—Young AI Leaders Austria hosted “AI Skills Training: Empowering Communities for the Future,” with peers from the Viseu hub and international partners.

## What happened

Catalyst Now has grown into a network of 5,500+ changemakers across 65+ countries. The webinar explored how AI literacy becomes a foundation for readiness and resilience, referencing the AI Literacy Framework (2025 draft) by the European Commission and OECD: technical knowledge, durable skills, and future-ready attitudes.

Speakers included Maria João Martins (Viseu Hub), Viktor Loreth (Linz hub), Vedant Ahluwalia (Dataviv / Stanford Angels India), and Tijana Zivkovic (WorkWise Academy). Discussion drew on WEF’s finding that 40% of workforce skills will change within five years, and on research showing many Gen Z learners still struggle to spot AI shortfalls such as fabricated facts.

## Why it matters

Without intentional frameworks, “AI education” risks becoming superficial. Communities and companies need structured literacy—so AI empowers people rather than creating dependency.
